A Markham Auto Shop Built on Trust

Pronto Automotive has been serving Markham and the surrounding area since 1979. We specialize in the diagnosis and repair of gasoline and diesel vehicles, from light duty to heavy duty.

Whether you need an oil change or an engine change, our goal is courteous, professional service of the highest calibre — and workmanship we’re prepared to stand behind.

At Pronto, the customer always comes first. We’ll be by your side from the first call to long after the repair.
