Tire Service in Markham

Tires are the only part of your vehicle that touches the road. We help you choose the right tires, install and balance them properly, and keep them lined up so they wear evenly.

Call 905-294-9476

What it is

Tread depth, pressure, balance and alignment all affect grip, braking distance and how long tires last. In Ontario, seasonal tires make a real difference to safety when the weather turns.

Signs you should book a visit

  • Worn or uneven tread
  • Vibration at highway speed
  • Vehicle pulling to one side
  • Frequent low tire pressure
  • Time for a seasonal changeover

What we can help with

  • Tire supply and installation
  • Mounting and balancing
  • Seasonal tire changeovers
  • Wheel alignment
  • Tires for cars, trucks and fleet vehicles

Work we stand behind

We’ll inspect your vehicle, explain what we find in plain language and walk you through your options before any work begins. If something isn’t right, we work with you to make it right.
