Exhaust & Muffler Repair in Markham

A loud or leaking exhaust is more than an annoyance. We inspect and repair exhaust systems, mufflers and related emission parts on gas and diesel vehicles.

Call 905-294-9476

What it is

The exhaust system carries hot gases away from the engine and out from under the vehicle, quiets engine noise and cleans up emissions through the catalytic converter. Road salt and moisture rust exhaust parts over time, and a leak can let fumes into the cabin.

Signs you should book a visit

  • Louder than normal engine or rumbling noise
  • Rattling from underneath the vehicle
  • Exhaust smell inside the cabin
  • Hanging or dragging exhaust pipe
  • Check engine light related to emissions
  • Drop in fuel economy

What we can help with

  • Exhaust inspection
  • Muffler and pipe repair or replacement
  • Hangers, clamps and gaskets
  • Catalytic converter diagnosis
  • Emission testing
  • Exhaust work on fleet and diesel vehicles

Work we stand behind

We’ll inspect your vehicle, explain what we find in plain language and walk you through your options before any work begins. If something isn’t right, we work with you to make it right.
